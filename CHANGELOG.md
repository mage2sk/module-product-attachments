## [1.1.10] - 2026-10-07

### Changed
- Storefront greys follow the store palette: the Clear Search & Filters button has a white background, a #D4D4D4 border and #525252 text with a teal hover (on Luma it was #333333 text with a #CCCCCC border), and the file preview popup text, borders and backgrounds are #171717, #525252, #E5E5E5 and #FAFAFA (were #333333, #666666, #999999, #BDC3C7, #DDDDDD and #F9F9F9).
